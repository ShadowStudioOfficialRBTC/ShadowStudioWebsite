01 / about us

Passionate about robotics, curious about everything.

Shadow Studios is a creative robotics platform for people who like to take things apart, understand how they work, and build something more surprising.

From tiny sensors to autonomous systems, we blend code, electronics, design, and a little stubborn optimism. Every project is a chance to learn in public and make the next idea possible.
